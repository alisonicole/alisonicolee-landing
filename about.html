I build things. Products, communities, tools, frameworks, the occasional clearer way of thinking about something. I am drawn to the messy middle: when there is something real to work with but no one has figured out what to do with it yet.

At BCG I work on applied AI, which mostly means figuring out what AI can actually do inside organizations rather than what people say it can do. Before BCG I spent four years at P&G running a $30M to $40M portfolio, growing it by 28 points over target and winning the annual Dish Care Category Award along the way. I hold an MBA from Wharton, where I was EVP of Student Leadership and a William P. Lauder Leadership Fellow.

On building

I started vibe-coding tools to scratch my own itches. The Values Inventory came from wanting a more honest way to figure out what I actually cared about. Between came from trying to get more out of therapy. Greenhouse came from the aftermath of something hard. The tools exist because I needed them and could not find them.

On coaching

All In exists because I kept having the same conversation. Talented people with great resumes who were good at the game and not sure they wanted to keep playing it. I do not give generic career advice. I help people get honest with themselves about what is actually next and build toward it on purpose.

On AI

The gap is not between people who can use AI and people who cannot. It is between people who know what to ask and people who are just prompting into the void. I think a lot about how to close that gap at the individual and organizational level. It is the most interesting problem I have worked on.
