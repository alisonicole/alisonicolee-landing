The path

Sales to business school to consulting to AI. And the doors that someone opened for me along the way.

  1. 2018 · P&G

    I wanted to learn sales, because every business runs on it. (It was also the first stock my grandmother ever bought.) I built an Excel tool that automated a daily task from half-a-day to minutes, and fifty people ended up using it. I didn't call it building yet.

  2. 2022 · Wharton

    I wanted more than my slice of CPG.

    Door: my managers, who wrote my recommendations.

  3. 2024 · BCG

    I wanted to see more industries. Consulting taught me how to break a problem down and hold my own in the rooms where the big decisions get made.

    Door: my classmates, who prepped me for every case.

  4. 2026 · Sideways into AI

    A friend showed me an app he'd built with Claude Code. I signed up that day and built my first one on the side. When I showed it to a colleague at BCG, he said, “you know I do this at BCG, right?” I had no idea that was an option. I joined Applied AI, shipped an agent that's now deployed across ~500 cases a year, and made Project Leader.

    Doors: my friend, my colleague, and my employer.

  5. 2026 · Scribe

    I posted about becoming Applied AI Lead and startups started reaching out. I left consulting because I wanted to work at an AI-native company, close to the frontier, with real impact.

    Door: I opened this one myself - I posted my work, and a recruiter found me.

So many of these doors were opened by other people. Now I try to hold them open for others.
