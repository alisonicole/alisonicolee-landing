About

Hi, I'm
Alison.

I'm the Founding AI Transformation Strategist at Scribe, helping companies figure out what to actually build with AI and how to get it into the hands of the people who need it.

I came at AI sideways. Not through a CS degree, through strategy. I spent two years at BCG on pricing, cost optimization, and transformation, and shipped applied AI products through the Helix team. Before BCG I owned five category portfolios at P&G and grew them all above target. I have an MBA from Wharton.

I vibe-code small apps on the side to stay close to where AI is actually going, not where people say it's going. I write about what I learn: what it takes to go from zero to building, what gets easier, and what doesn't.
